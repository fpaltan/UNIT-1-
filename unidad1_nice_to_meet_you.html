Conversation 1

Mr. Miller: Hello! I'm your teacher. My name is Mr. Miller.

Robert: Good morning, Mr. Miller.

Mr. Miller: Good morning. What's your name?

Robert: My name is Robert Brent.

Mr. Miller: This is Ana Cruz. She's a student. She is in our class.

Robert: Nice to meet you, Ana.

Ana: Nice to meet you, too, Robert.

Ver respuestas: Choose the correct answer

1. Robert Brent is a student. · 2. The teacher is Mr. Miller. · 3. Ana Cruz is a student.

Practice the conversation with two classmates.
